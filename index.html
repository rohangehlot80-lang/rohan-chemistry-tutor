Teaching Methodology

How I Teach Chemistry

A structured, proven approach that builds deep understanding — not just surface-level recall.

1

Concept First

Before any problem-solving, I ensure the underlying concept is fully understood — using real-world examples and visual analogies that make abstract ideas tangible.

2

Step-by-Step Problem Solving

I walk through problems systematically, building a logical framework students can apply independently to any question — not just the ones we practiced.

3

Visual Learning

Diagrams, molecular models, reaction maps, and mind-maps make even the most complex Organic mechanisms visually intuitive and memorable.

4

Exam Strategy

Past papers, mark-scheme analysis, and time-management tactics tailored specifically to AP, IB, A-Level, and NEET/JEE exam formats and marking criteria.

FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before booking.

Which curricula and subjects do you teach?
+
I teach Chemistry across AP (US), IB SL & HL (International), IGCSE (Cambridge & Edexcel), A-Level (AQA, OCR, Edexcel), and NEET & JEE (India). All sessions are conducted online, so I can work with students from anywhere in the world.
Do you teach complete beginners?
+
Absolutely. Whether you're starting Chemistry from scratch or picking up after a long break, I build the foundation you need before progressing to more advanced topics. No prior knowledge is assumed — we start from where you are.
Do you help with AP exam preparation?
+
Yes — AP exam prep is one of my core offerings. I cover the full AP Chemistry curriculum (Units 1–9), work through past FRQs and MCQs, and use targeted strategies to help students score 4s and 5s. I also focus on lab-based questions and the specific thinking skills the AP exam demands.
Do you provide notes and assignments?
+
Yes. After each session I provide clear, structured notes summarizing key concepts covered. I also assign relevant practice problems and past paper questions for homework. Everything is tailored to your specific syllabus and current level.
What is your teaching style like?
+
I take a concept-first approach: I make sure you truly understand the "why" behind every idea before moving on. Sessions are interactive — not lectures. I use visual aids, real-life examples, and frequent check-ins. The pace is entirely adapted to you, and I encourage questions at every step.
How are sessions conducted?
+
All sessions are conducted online via Zoom or Google Meet, using a digital whiteboard for working through problems visually. Sessions are typically 60 or 90 minutes, scheduled flexibly to accommodate different time zones across the US, UK, and India.
How do I get started?
+
Simply fill out the booking form above, or reach out via WhatsApp or email. I'll respond within 24 hours to schedule a free trial session. The trial is a full 45-minute lesson — no strings attached — so you can experience the teaching approach before committing.
